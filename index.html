# every pixel is a letter

      

        Underneath, Glyphborne is a real 3D world. You never see it directly. Each frame is
        rendered off-screen, sliced into tiny cells, and every cell is replaced by one coloured
        character chosen for its brightness, its direction and what it is made of.
      

      
  +--------------+      +---------------+      +----------------+      +--------------+
  |   3D scene   | ---> |  6 x 9 px     | ---> |  pick a glyph  | ---> |  tint it and |
  |  knights,    |      |  cells        |      |  by brightness |      |  draw on     |
  |  grass, fire |      |  across the   |      |  and material  |      |  black       |
  +--------------+      |  screen       |      +----------------+      +--------------+
                        +---------------+
      grass    -->  , ' | / \          stone  -->  . o O 0 # @
      leaves   -->  c e o s w m B &    water  -->  - ~ = + *
      armour   -->  . : ; I l ! [ ] { } X Q 0 M W &

      

        Zoom out and the characters shrink with the camera, so the wide view resolves into a
        finer, almost painted picture. Zoom in and the knight becomes a crowd of letters.
      

    


    

      
# what's inside

      

        

          
  \|/ \|/
 \|/ \|/ \|/

          
A living meadow

          
Tens of thousands of grass blades sway in the wind and part around your boots. Fireflies drift, braziers crackle and the pond holds the moon.

        

        

          
    /
 o-[==]---

          
Real-time combat

          
Swing, crit and watch the numbers fly. Skeleton warriors wander in camps, hunt you on sight, crumble when beaten and rise again later.

        

        

          
   _/
  /_
   /

          
Spells and potions

          
Whirlwind through a crowd, call lightning down on a target, mend your wounds, and keep a potion ready for when the fight turns.

        

        

          
  /\/\/\
 ( x  x )

          
The Hollow King

          
Clear the glade and the ground shudders. A giant crowned skeleton rises in the ruins. Watch for the red ring of his ground slam.

        

        

          
 [-]  [+]
  zoom

          
Zoom for detail

          
Pull the camera back and the glyphs get finer. Pick your preferred detail level, from chunky retro terminal to fine-grained print.

        

        

          
 [ save ]
  ======

          
Picks up where you left off

          
Your level, gold, potions and quest progress save automatically in your browser. Close the tab, come back, carry on.

        

      

    


    

      
# how to play

      

        
  +---------------+----------------------+
  | W A S D       | move                 |
  | Shift         | run                  |
  | Space / click | sword strike         |
  | 1             | Whirlwind   (20 mp)  |
  | 2             | Lightning   (30 mp)  |
  | 3             | Mend        (35 mp)  |
  | F             | drink a potion       |
  | Q / E         | turn the camera      |
  | wheel / + -   | zoom (finer glyphs)  |
  | [ / ]         | glyph size           |
  | Esc           | menu and settings    |
  +---------------+----------------------+

        

          
Your quest

          
1. Destroy twelve skeleton warriors across Ashenmere Glade. They gather in small camps; lone ones are easier.

          
2. When the ground shudders, head west to the ruins. The Hollow King waits by the braziers.

          
3. When his blade goes up, a red ring marks where it will land. Get out of it, then punish him with lightning.

          
Levelling up raises your health, mana and strength. Skeletons sometimes drop potions.
